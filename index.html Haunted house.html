Our Story

A House With a Past We Invented on Purpose

Haunted House began with a simple idea: what if the warmth of a Bangladeshi kitchen lived inside a building full of shadows? Not a haunted attraction, and not a themed novelty — a real restaurant, dressed in atmosphere, built around real food.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries the same hospitality you'd find in a Banani home — kacchi steaming under a sealed lid, bhorta pounded by hand, fish simmered in mustard. The mansion is theatre. The food is the truth.

We built this place for the people who want a dinner to feel like an occasion — couples, families, old friends, and anyone curious enough to knock on a door that glows gold at night.
